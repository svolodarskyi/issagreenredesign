Our Story

Calgary Based A Japanese Organic
Tea Brand

ISSA GREEN was founded by Kazuki Nomura, a Japanese fabric designer who moved to Canada and started this business to promote sustainability, organic-based products and connecting independent farmers with consumers willing to try healthy and tasty Japanese tea.

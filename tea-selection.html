Fukamushi sencha

Sugimoto farm in Shizuoka
Health&Beauty Regular caffeine Rich flavor

ISSA GREEN's signature tea.

This sencha is produced by steaming leaves two to three times longer than regular varieties — creating a uniquely rich, smooth cup.

More about Fukamushi Sencha +

The steaming process makes tea leaves finer, and insoluble nutrition (fiber, protein, β-carotene, vitamin E, chlorophyll and minerals) is released from the powdery part of the tea.

Rich in catechin, vitamin C, vitamin B2, tannin, flavonoid, gamma-aminobutyric acid, pectin, saponin, polysaccharide, fluorine and caffeine.

We recommend a cup of fukamushi tea in the morning for immune support and a beauty booster!

Matcha

Matcha Most Popular

Ishikawa farm in Toyota, Aichi
Health&Beauty Energy boost Rich flavor

Because it is difficult to obtain genuine matcha outside of Japan, we are especially proud to offer this genuine matcha grown by a specialist farmer using organic methods.

Available in two ceremonial grades — both significantly superior to culinary grade.

More about Matcha +

We carry two ceremonial grades (not culinary grade!). Ceremonial matcha has richness, maturity and a slight natural sweetness. Fine matcha has freshness, bold character and clear bitterness — excellent for lattes.

Matcha is known as a Japanese super food: rich in catechin, β-carotene, vitamin E, K, B vitamins, vitamin C, folic acid, niacin and minerals. Popular for immune support and anti-aging worldwide.

Called the Japanese espresso — high in caffeine, great for starting your day.

How to Brew +

1 tsp / 70–100 ml

For sweetness, brew at lower temperature.
